How to play

Player

  • Move: drag the joystick on the console, or use WASD / the arrow keys.
  • Use (USE button or E): open or close the drawer, door or cushion you're facing and search it. The highlighted outline shows which one. Corner drawers: push diagonally into the corner.
  • Drop (DROP button): drop the item you're carrying. Nothing to carry yet in stage 1.
  • Sneak (SNEAK button): move quietly. Coming later.
  • Shoot (SHOOT button): stun gun. Coming later; the button is greyed out.
  • Locked doors tell you so when you press Use next to them.

Phantom (stage 2, placeholder)

  • Haunt the house and hunt the players before they escape.
  • Move with the same joystick or WASD / arrow keys.
  • Phantom powers and their buttons will be announced in stage 2.
